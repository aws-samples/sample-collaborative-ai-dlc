# My work

**My work** (in the sidebar, above **Spaces**) lists the questions waiting for an answer in every space you belong to, so you don't have to open each intent to find them. The Spaces dashboard stays the landing page.

## What it lists

Every pending human task of a running or waiting intent in your spaces:

- agent questions and checkpoints;
- engine approvals and review verdicts;
- stage reviews.

Only the current run and stage attempt count. A question that was answered, cancelled, or replaced by a rewind or a retried stage disappears. Questions from parallel lanes are listed separately, with their unit lane and section.

Each row shows the space, the intent, a short summary, the stage and lane, the type, and how long it has been waiting (hover the age for the exact time).

## Open to eligible members

Every row carries the label **Open to eligible members**. Any member of the space can answer these questions today, so the list shows what is open to you, not what is assigned to you. Someone else may answer first.

## Ordering and filters

Questions are listed oldest first. Use **Load more** to see further questions.

- **Space** limits the list to one space.
- **Type** shows only **Questions** (agent questions and checkpoints) or **Approvals and reviews** (approvals, review verdicts and stage reviews).

Filters are kept in the address, so reloading the page or going back keeps them. If you lose access to the filtered space, the page says so and shows all your spaces.

## Partial results

If a space can't be checked, an amber banner names it and says its questions may be missing. This happens when a read fails, or when a space has too many active intents to check at once. **Retry** checks again. The page never says "Nothing is waiting for your answer" while any space is missing; it says "No questions found in the spaces that could be checked" instead.

## Freshness

The page loads fresh each time you open it. It also refreshes when you return to the browser tab, when the network comes back, every 60 seconds while the tab is visible, and with **Refresh**. It doesn't need a live connection.

## Answering

**Open question** takes you to the intent, with that question selected, or to the stage review page for a stage review. You answer there, as usual; My work doesn't answer in place. If the question was answered or replaced in the meantime, the intent page says it is no longer waiting, and any other open questions stay below.

## Current limits

My work lists questions only. Review requests and notification history aren't included yet, and there is no dismiss, snooze or count badge.
